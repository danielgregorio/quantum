# The Robot assets

From the "Platformer 2D" demo in godotengine/godot-demo-projects
(`2d/platformer`, MIT, copyright 2014-present Godot Engine contributors and
2007-2014 Juan Linietsky, Ariel Manzur):

- `tiles.png`, `robot.png`, `enemy.png`, `coin.png`, `bullet.png` and
  `platform.png` are the demo's `.webp` images, converted to PNG unchanged.
- `jump.wav`, `shoot.wav`, `coin_pickup.wav` and `explode.wav` are the demo's
  sounds.

The demo's music ("Pompy" by Hubert Lamontagne) is not included: the demo
credits it without a license of its own.
