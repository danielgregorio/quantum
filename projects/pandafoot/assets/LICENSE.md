# The PandaFoot assets

`frame.png` (a box of a DOS screen, a double line in grey on blue: a 24x24
nine-slice picture) is drawn for this game, and `goal.ogg` (a crowd roaring a
goal), `whistle.ogg` (the referee's whistle) and `final.ogg` (the final
whistle) are synthesized for it, by `scripts/art/pandafoot.py`: CC0, no
attribution needed.

`DejaVuSansMono.ttf` is DejaVu Sans Mono, of the DejaVu fonts
(https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/): Bitstream Vera, copyright (c) 2003 by
Bitstream, Inc., with the DejaVu changes in the public domain, under the
licence in `DejaVu-LICENSE.txt`, next to it.
