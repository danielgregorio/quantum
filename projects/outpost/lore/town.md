Outpost is a frontier town of a few hundred souls, walled with a palisade of pine. Its temple stands in the
north-west of the town; whoever falls in the wild wakes at its altar. Its gate faces east, onto the road to the
forest and, north, to the cave.

The sewers under the town are full of rats. They bite, but they are slow, and a newcomer can clear them.

The forest east of the gate is the wolves'. At night there are twice as many. Their furs keep the town warm in
winter.

The cave north-east is the Old Bear's. It sleeps until something comes near, and no one goes in alone.
