# The Creeps assets

From the "Dodge the Creeps" demo in godotengine/godot-demo-projects
(`2d/dodge_the_creeps`, MIT, copyright 2017 KidsCanCode), which credits them:

- `player.png` and `creeps.png` are the demo's frames packed into sheets by
  `scripts/pack-sprites.py` (creeps at 0.75, the scale the demo draws them at).
  The images are from "Abstract Platformer", created in 2016 by kenney.nl,
  CC0 1.0 Universal. Source: https://www.kenney.nl/assets/abstract-platformer
- `music.ogg` is "House In a Forest Loop", copyright 2012 HorrorPen,
  CC-BY 3.0 Attribution. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/loop-house-in-a-forest
- `gameover.wav` is the demo's, MIT.
- `../fonts/Xolonium-Regular.ttf` is "Xolonium", copyright 2011-2016 Severin
  Meyer, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`../fonts/LICENSE.txt`).
