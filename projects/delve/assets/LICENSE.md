# The Delve assets

`crypt.png` (floor, wall, wall with a torch, lever, pulled lever, gem, heart,
chest, stairs, crusher, bones, rubble: 18x18 tiles) and `delvers.png` (two
hooded delvers walking and swinging in four directions, a skeleton, a bat:
24x24 frames) are drawn for this game by `scripts/art/delve.py`: CC0, no
attribution needed. The sounds are Kenney's Pixel Platformer (CC0), in
`assets/kenney/` at the top of the repository.

`torchlight.ogg` (the title and the end) and `crypt.ogg` (the crypts) are
written and synthesized for this game by `scripts/art/delve_music.py`: CC0
as well.
