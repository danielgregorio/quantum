# The Chess assets

`pieces.png` packs the twelve piece images of github.com/Prashanna135/chess
(MIT), which that repository credits to Kenney (kenney.nl, CC0) — see
`kenney-license.txt`, the licence file shipped with them. `board.png` is drawn
for this repository (scripts in the commit history; CC0): eight 62x62 frames —
the light and the dark square, the picked square, a square the picked piece
may go to, a piece it may take, the cursor, the last move, a king in check.
