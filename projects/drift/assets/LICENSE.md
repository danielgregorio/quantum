# The Drift assets

`ships.png` (the ship, drones, tanks and shots, 24x24 frames), `boss.png`
(calm and angry, 40x40) and `space.png` (the sky) are drawn for this game by
`scripts/art/drift.py`: CC0, no attribution needed. The sounds are Kenney's
Pixel Platformer (CC0), in `assets/kenney/` at the top of the repository.
