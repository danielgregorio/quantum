# The Siege assets

`ground.png` (grass, grass with flowers, the keep's flagstones and its wall:
16x16 tiles) and `siege.png` (the archer, the knight on a horse, a raider, a
brute, an arrow, a bolt, a ram, the tower and the aim's cross: 16x16 frames)
are drawn for this game by `scripts/art/siege.py`: CC0, no attribution
needed. The sounds are Kenney's Pixel Platformer (CC0), in `assets/kenney/`
at the top of the repository.
