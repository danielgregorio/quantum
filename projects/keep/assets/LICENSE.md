# The Keep assets

`dungeon.png` (floor, wall, door, gate, switch, sign, chest, key, heart:
18x18 tiles) and `hero.png` (the hero walking and swinging in four directions,
a slime, a bat: 24x24 frames) are drawn for this game by `scripts/art/keep.py`:
CC0, no attribution needed. The sounds are Kenney's Pixel Platformer (CC0), in
`assets/kenney/` at the top of the repository.
