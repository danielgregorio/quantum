# The Bricks assets

`bricks.png` (five colours of brick, a steel brick whole and cracked, a bomb
brick: 32x12 frames), `paddles.png` (the four players' paddles: 48x8 frames)
and `ball.png` (8x8) are drawn for this game by `scripts/art/bricks.py`: CC0,
no attribution needed. The sounds are Kenney's Pixel Platformer (CC0), in
`assets/kenney/` at the top of the repository.
